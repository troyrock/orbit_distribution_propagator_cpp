How to interpret this visualization

Coverage is operational and depends on the ensemble size, angular bins, thresholds, and saved-epoch cadence. A finite Monte Carlo sample cannot prove positive probability at every angle. An angularly broad cloud may retain radial and vertical structure or coherent correlations. True anomaly need not be uniformly distributed even when mean orbital phase is well mixed.

Focus cloud automatically recenters and rescales each saved epoch. Whole orbit uses one fixed scale across the run. The curve is an instantaneous reference orbit, not the trajectory of an individual sample. Dim particles behind Earth remain visible when Hidden points is enabled. Colors track stable sample identities and do not indicate probability density.
